01Who We Are

Who We Are

We are a collective of Project Managers and Programme Managers who have extensive experience and knowledge delivering infrastructure projects across major Tier 1 investment institutions.

  • 30+ Years’ combined experience as former technologists
  • 30,000 Users — the upper range of our Windows 10 deployments across multiple regions
  • PRINCE2 And AgilePM accredited — and Scrum Masters too
  • Tier 1 Investment institutions served on infrastructure delivery

01.1

Knowledge and insight

Our project managers have not become project managers overnight. We are former technologists with over 30 years’ combined experience and knowledge, gained from being on the other side as systems administrators, network engineers and database administrators.

We have supported, developed and maintained your environment across different platforms and technologies. That allows us to apply proper project management methodologies to deliver your projects successfully, on time and on budget — while understanding the common constraints and challenges of delivering complex projects to meet your business outcomes and benefits.

01.2

Agile or Waterfall or Lean?

We have working, proven experience across these methodologies. We are all PRINCE2 accredited and AgilePM accredited. We are Scrum Masters too!

We keep abreast of current working practices by leveraging methodologies that suit your organisation and adapt to your own internal governance processes.
