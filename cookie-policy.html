Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Cookie Policy explains the use of cookies and similar technologies in connection with alisoveijo.co.uk. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and other methods of accessing or storing information on a device. They may support essential delivery and security functions, preferences or measurement.

2. Our website's approach

The Aliso Veijo website is designed as a lightweight informational website. The core website does not require advertising cookies or analytics tracking to display its pages. The website may nevertheless rely on hosting or security technology needed to serve pages and protect against abuse.

CategoryHow it is used
Essential website delivery and securityHosting and security infrastructure may use strictly necessary technologies to deliver pages, maintain availability and prevent malicious activity.
AnalyticsThe redesigned static website does not include optional analytics as part of its core implementation.
Advertising and marketingThe redesigned static website does not include advertising or marketing trackers as part of its core implementation.
ContactContact enquiries may be sent to us using the contact information on the website. Any future online submission service will be assessed separately for its data and storage practices.

3. Consent and your choices

Under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), some uses of cookies and similar technologies require consent before they are used. Certain purposes are exempt, including technologies strictly necessary to provide a service requested by the user. Other statutory exceptions have their own conditions, which may include an easy way to object. Non-essential technology requiring consent will not be enabled without an appropriate choice mechanism.

4. Managing website data

You can view, block or remove cookies and site data using your browser settings. Restricting necessary security or delivery technologies may affect how a website works. Consult your browser's help documentation for instructions.

5. Third-party infrastructure

Hosting, security and communications services can involve third-party processing. Their precise use of browser storage depends on the services and security settings in operation. See our Privacy Policy for information about how personal information is handled.

6. Updates

We will revise this policy if the website introduces new technologies or our use of cookies and similar technologies changes.
