Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ALISO VEIJO LIMITED (trading as Aliso Veijo) collects and uses personal information when you visit alisoveijo.co.uk or contact us about our project and programme management consultancy services.

1. Data controller and contact

The data controller is ALISO VEIJO LIMITED, company number 08233322, 72 Eastcote Lane, Northolt, UB5 5RQ, United Kingdom. For data protection enquiries, rights requests or complaints, contact paulo.pina@alisoveijo.co.uk.

2. Personal information we handle

If you contact us, we may handle your name, business name, email address, telephone number if provided, message and subsequent correspondence. When the website is accessed, technical information such as IP address, browser details, access times and security events may be processed by our hosting infrastructure to deliver and protect the website.

We ask that you do not include confidential or special-category personal information in a general enquiry unless necessary.

3. Purposes and legal bases

PurposeUK GDPR lawful basis
Responding to enquiries and discussing consultancy servicesLegitimate interests in handling business enquiries; or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, where applicable
Managing client relationships and agreed servicesPerformance of a contract where applicable, and legitimate interests in maintaining business communications
Operating, securing and troubleshooting the websiteLegitimate interests in reliable, secure website operation
Meeting legal and regulatory obligationsCompliance with a legal obligation

Our legitimate interests include communicating with potential and existing clients, maintaining business records and protecting the website from misuse. A business enquiry does not itself constitute consent to receive marketing emails.

4. Sharing information

Personal information is accessible to authorised people acting for the company and may be handled by service providers involved in website hosting, security, business email and professional support. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information if required by law or in connection with the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

5. Transfers outside the UK

Service providers may handle information outside the UK. Where a restricted transfer takes place, it must be covered by a valid transfer mechanism, such as applicable UK adequacy regulations or appropriate contractual safeguards. Information about a particular transfer can be requested using the contact details above.

6. Retention

We retain enquiries and business correspondence for as long as needed to deal with the enquiry, administer any resulting business relationship, comply with applicable record-keeping obligations and resolve disputes. Relevant accounting and contractual records may be retained for longer where required by law. Technical records are retained for the period needed for operational and security purposes, subject to the configuration of the hosting provider. When personal information is no longer needed, it is deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

7. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to be informed, access your information, correct inaccuracies, request erasure, restrict processing, object to processing and request data portability. If we rely on consent for a particular activity, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing.

You have the right to object to direct marketing at any time. To exercise your rights, email paulo.pina@alisoveijo.co.uk. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

8. Data protection complaints

If you believe we have not handled your personal information properly, you can make a complaint to us using the email address above. We will acknowledge a data protection complaint within 30 days, make appropriate enquiries and respond without undue delay. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

See our Cookie Policy for information about cookies, browser storage and applicable choices.

10. Children

Our website is intended for business and professional enquiries and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us.

11. External websites

External websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices. Please review their notices before submitting information.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our services, website technologies or legal obligations change. The latest version will be available on this page.
